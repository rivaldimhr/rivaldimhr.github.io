Re: Application for Data Analyst

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to apply for the Data Analyst position. I recently graduated in Information Technology Systems from Institut Teknologi Bandung, and I enjoy turning raw data into answers that a business can act on.

During my internship at PT Sarana Pactindo, I built a web-based billing forecast system and analyzed data to support updates to two company websites, working with two stakeholders to understand what they needed and to improve the accuracy of the results. My two Quality Assurance roles since then taught me to check numbers carefully before anyone relies on them.

In my own projects, I analyzed 98,000 e-commerce orders with 13 SQL queries covering cohort retention, RFM segmentation and delivery delays. I found that only 3% of customers buy again and that a late first delivery lowers repeat purchases by 25% (p = 0.002), and I turned this into five recommendations with success metrics. I also built a 7-day demand forecast for 300 products from 1 million retail transactions. Both projects have public dashboards and code on my portfolio.

I would bring solid SQL and Python, a habit of testing my own analysis, and the ability to explain findings in plain language to people outside the data team. I would welcome the chance to discuss how I could help your team make better decisions with data. Thank you for your time and consideration.
